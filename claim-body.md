A black-and-white business-card handout to give someone when you pay them in cash. Standard size: 1050 x 600 px at 300 dpi (3.5 x 2.0 in); the print PDF has two pages at exactly 252 x 144 pt, no bleed, nothing to trim.

FRONT - banknote framing in one ink: "SERIES C · 2026" and a serial number at the top, then the hook "THIS NOTE / LEAVES NO TRACE.", two plain sentences ("No bank. No app. No record of you." / "Works when power, network or banks are down."), a solid band reading "PRIVATE · OFFLINE · FINAL · YOURS - TURN OVER", and a "FROM ______" rule so whoever hands it over can write their name on it.

BACK - "WHY I PAID YOU IN CASH", headline "What a card can't do", and four numbered points that answer the payment card in the reader's pocket directly:
01 PRIVATE - no bank, no app, no name.
02 OFFLINE - still pays with the power out.
03 FINAL - settled at once, no chargeback.
04 YOURS - no account, no limit, no asking.
Full-width call to action: "NEXT TIME: ASK FOR YOUR CHANGE IN CASH."

WHY THIS DESIGN: the argument is the payment itself. The card is written to be read in four seconds by the person who has just been handed a banknote, and every line answers something a payment card does badly. The banknote framing (serial, series, solid denomination band) makes it read as money, not as a flyer.

PRINT: card_print.pdf at 100 percent, no fit-to-page, on heavy stock, duplex flipped on the short edge. Pure K black on white, no greys, no colour, nothing to recolour.
